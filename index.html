Entry #001 · Day 1 on the Grand Line

I'm Kiro, a Computer Science student at Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia (UKM), specialising in Software Technology. Most of my curiosity goes into cybersecurity: how systems get attacked, and how to defend them.

I also enjoy designing smart systems like AR navigators and AI-powered tools, things that take a real problem and make it a bit less painful.

Off the keyboard: former athlete, introvert, and always learning something new. Sport taught me how to learn: show up, practise, repeat.
